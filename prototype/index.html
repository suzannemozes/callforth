About

Call Forth meets artists at the threshold of the blank page, the cold studio, the loud critic who arrives before we begin. We want to move you through that threshold in under five minutes to bring you to your work with a fresh mind and a calm nervous system. Instead of cobbling together multiple tools to arrive at the same place and using up energy in the process, this is a smooth choose-your-own-adventure to work with your resistance instead of against it.

When we're tired, stressed or unsure what we want, a long menu works against us. In a well-known study, shoppers offered 24 jams were far less likely to buy one than shoppers offered 6. Later reviews found that too many options hurt most when a choice feels hard and we don't know what we prefer, which is exactly how a block feels.

So Call Forth makes the small decisions for you: one breath, one move, and only two other ways in if you need them. The less time you spend here, the better it's working.

Sources: Iyengar & Lepper (2000), "When choice is demotivating," Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. Chernev, Böckenholt & Goodman (2015), "Choice overload: a conceptual review and meta-analysis," Journal of Consumer Psychology.

Suzanne Mozes

Suzanne spent years in magazines writing fluently in every voice but her own, until she went to Columbia University School of the Arts to find it and discovered how much she loves to teach.

A writer, editor and visual artist with more than 25 years of craft, she helps brilliant minds shape what only they can make. She built Call Forth so her students' best moves would be there at 2am too.
