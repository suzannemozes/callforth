### App Brief

- **Name and promise:** Call Forth. Pick what's in the way. Walk through it. Make the thing.
- **Type:** Standalone freemium app. Free to start; full studio $27 one-time for the first [100] founding members, then $47.
- **Who it's for:** Writers, artists, musicians and makers who keep losing to the blank page, the mood or the critic.
- **The one job:** Get a creator from blocked to making in minutes, matched to what's in the way today.
- **Design principle:** spend as little time in the app as possible. The person makes one real choice (their block); Call Forth makes the rest. Goal: from opening the app to the timer running in two taps and under two minutes.
- **Ease rules (from opening the app to the timer, with confidence):** returning artists open straight to Home (stay signed in on the device; sign in by emailed link, no password; installable on the phone's home screen and quick to load on a weak connection) · the breath starts by itself after a 3-second countdown · the move page first shows "Get your work open first.", then three short lines to clear the work space (tap to move through them faster; the tap line appears after 6 seconds), then one choice: straight to a work block, or a five-minute warm-up first · the screen stays awake for the whole session · on phones each step's main button sits at the bottom, in thumb reach · gentle fades between steps (off for reduced motion) · "Straight to making" for days they arrive ready · Suz's moves stay under 40 words, opening with a short instruction.
