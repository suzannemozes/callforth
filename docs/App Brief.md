# Call Forth: App Brief

The full written spec: every screen, rule, piece of content and tracked event. The prototype (prototype/index.html) shows the same thing working.


- **Name and promise:** Call Forth. Pick what's in the way. Walk through it. Make the thing.
- **Type:** Standalone freemium app. Free to start; full studio $27 one-time for the first [100] founding members, then $47.
- **Who it's for:** Writers, artists, musicians and makers who keep losing to the blank page, the mood or the critic.
- **The one job:** Get a creator from blocked to making in minutes, matched to what's in the way today.
- **Design principle:** spend as little time in the app as possible. The person makes one real choice (their block); Call Forth makes the rest. Goal: from opening the app to the timer running in two taps and under two minutes.
- **Ease rules (from opening the app to the timer, with confidence):** returning artists open straight to Home (stay signed in on the device; sign in by emailed link, no password; installable on the phone's home screen and quick to load on a weak connection) · the breath starts by itself after a 3-second countdown · the move page first shows "Get your work open first." on its own, then hands over to the prompt · the screen stays awake for the whole session · on phones each step's main button sits at the bottom, in thumb reach · gentle fades between steps (off for reduced motion) · "Straight to making" for days they arrive ready · Suz's moves stay under 40 words, opening with a short instruction.
- **First working version:** https://claude.ai/artifact/VH1qcHenhMRpKqCPX9Bz5L

4. **Home:** a greeting and a question that change every sign-on (see "Greetings and questions" below). Eight block cards: 3 free (no label), 5 studio (small label "Full studio" when locked, "Studio" for members; tapping a locked one goes to Studio). Under the question, one quiet italic line: "About two minutes from here to making." On every third visit (and only with log history on), one line from their own past log appears under that, in small italics with a bronze tag: "Rewrote the opening paragraph." YOU, LAST TUESDAY. Below the blocks, a verdigris "straight to making" link, a little larger than the other quiet labels: an italic Playfair phrase that changes every visit (never the same twice in a row) followed by "STRAIGHT TO MAKING →". The phrases: I feel pretty good today. · I'm in a good mood. · No complaints. · I've got oodles of energy. · Let's do this. · I've got this. 5. **Session (focus mode):** four steps with a step indicator. The menu disappears for the whole session; the header shows only the wordmark (not clickable) and a small "× Leave" pill at the top right. No upsells, locks or studio invitations appear inside a session. The screen stays awake for the whole session. On phones, each step's main button is pinned to the bottom of the screen. Steps fade in gently. The session pages never name the block they chose.
   - **Breathe:** one breath, already chosen (studio members who've had a breath help with this block get that breath, with one quiet line above it: "Last time, the [breath] helped."). Breath name, how-to (after they've done that breath 3 times, the how-to folds into a small "How to do it"), animated circle with a small progress bar. The circle and its progress bar share the timer's dawn-to-dusk colors: the circle starts blush and deepens round by round through ochre and sage toward dusk blue, softly lit from the upper left, so the breath and the making feel like one arc. The breath starts by itself: the circle shows a large 3, 2, 1 with "Breathing with you in 3…", then begins (no voice). The main button reads "Pause" (after pausing: "Begin again"), "Skip to my move →" (quiet label), and a small collapsed "Why this works" with sources. When the breath ends, it moves on to Your move by itself. No choosing between breaths. Circle words follow each breath: In / Sip / Hold / Out / Haaa / Hum / Whoosh / Release; alternate nostril shows "In · left", "Out · right" and so on. Each breath has its own number of rounds.
   - **Your move:** first, on its own, in large Playfair lettering: "Get your work open first." with a smaller italic line beneath, "The file, the sketchbook, the instrument." It holds for about 3.5 seconds, then fades out over half a second while the prompt fades in, with only about 0.3 seconds of overlap, so one block hands over to the next. Tapping the cue skips straight to the prompt; with reduced motion the swap is instant. Then the craft prompt for the block (under 40 words). The buttons stay in place the whole time. Main button: "My work is open. Start." (starts the timer immediately), or "Try something else".
   - **Something else (its own page):** "Two ways in. Pick one." Two large choice cards: one body option and one mind option, suited to their block (the page doesn't name it), never repeating what they've been offered this session. Free users choose from Another prompt, Mantra and Exercise reset; studio members from all 14. No locked cards, no reshuffle. Choosing a card opens its content below (audio in Suz's voice where it has some, instructions, then "When you're done, get your work open." and "My work is open. Start.", which starts the timer). "← Back to my move" (quiet label) sits at the top of the page. One line at the bottom: "Why only two? When you're stuck, fewer choices make it easier to start." (The full research note lives on the About page.)
   - **Make:** the timer is already running when they arrive, at their usual length (default 15 min) and sound. A warm singing-bowl tone marks the start (made in the browser, no sound file: a low 220 Hz note with gently beating partials, a slow 0.18-second bloom, a long 5-second fade and softened highs, played quietly). They can show the clock (big numbers) or the dawn-to-dusk bar: a wide band, about 64px tall, across the screen under "Make the thing." that fills left to right as time passes, its color shifting from blush through ochre and sage to dusk blue (#E9D3C6 → #E3C892 → #B9C49A → #8DBAB0 → #6E9FAE), with a soft feathered leading edge and a small italic line beneath ("9 min left"; hidden for people who've turned off their numbers, so the color alone tells the time). People who've turned off "Show my numbers" always get the bar. In the dark room the bar is gently dimmed. When time is up the same bowl sounds twice, softer, 1.6 seconds apart, the clock disappears, and the page says "Time." and "Keep going if you're in it." with Keep going (solid) and I'm done (outline). Nothing moves on by itself. Keep going switches to a quiet bronze count-up past the planned length, marked "Still going" (in bar view the bar stays full and the line beneath reads "Still going"); the log records the real minutes. Pause / Resume (solid) beside "I'm done" (outline). Below them, a status pill shows the current setting ("15 min · Silence ⌄"; free users see "15 min ⌄") and opens 5 / 15 / 25 min, (studio only) Silence / Rain on glass / Studio hum, and Show the clock / Show the bar; Call Forth remembers the choice.
   - **Log it:** "What did you make? A line or two, if you like." (optional text box), "How did it go? Tap to save." with three buttons in verdigris, most saturated on the left: It helped (#2E8A7E, light text) · A little (#9CCBC2) · Not today (#EEF4F2, nearly the plain button). On a phone the three sit in one row; one tap saves and opens the Daily light page. "Skip →" (quiet label) goes to the Daily light page without saving. No consult panel here.
   - **Daily light:** a full-screen page with only one short line of encouragement, different every day. Each line comes with its own bright color palette, typeface and moving shapes (sun, blobs, squiggles, confetti, moons, stars, waves, Matisse-like cutouts). No labels and no link to the log, so they stay in the flow. Under the line: "Would you like to set another timer?" with a button "Yes, another timer" (the timer starts right away for the same block) and an outline "Not now" button beside it (goes Home). The first time anyone ever saves a log, the line is always: "You're doing it. Really doing it. You showed up and did the work." No header or menu. Motion turns off for people who ask their device for reduced motion. This is the one room in the gallery where color is allowed.

**The logic:** the block chosen decides the suggested breath (sample pairing: teeth → Box breath; blank space → Vagal nerve breath; what comes next → Box breath; not a real artist → Lion's breath; not in the mood → Lion's breath; exhausted → Bumble bee hum; overstimulated → Bumble bee hum; understimulated → Lion's breath; later door "inner critic" → Bumble bee hum [Suz to confirm]), the move and the "something else" content. Prompts adapt to the discipline chosen at registration, with writing as the deepest track. Free vs studio decides which blocks, which "something else" options can be offered, learned breaths, log history, log filtering and sounds. Upsells only ever appear on Home (greyed blocks), the Studio page, and as quiet "with the full studio" notes on the Log filter and the Studio hours setting. "What helped most" for a block = the breath and the step that appear most often in that person's sessions for that block rated "It helped" (if none are rated, all sessions for that block). Consult rule: count visits (app opens). Before visit 10, never show the consult invitation. From visit 10, on each visit there's a random chance (about 1 in 4) of showing it, but only if it hasn't been shown yet this calendar month; once shown, it stays visible on the Log page and Studio page for that visit, then waits for the next month. The pep talk opens with the person's practice line ("You've been at your painting for 12 years. You know how to do this.").

**Why studio hours work:** deciding ahead of time when and where you'll act ("implementation intentions") reliably raises follow-through; Gollwitzer & Sheeran's 2006 meta-analysis of 94 studies found a medium-to-large effect (https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1).
